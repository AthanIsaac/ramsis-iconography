This folder is ready for your icon samples!

To add your icon images:
1. Upload JPG, PNG, or WEBP files to this folder
2. Update the Gallery.js component to reference your actual images
3. Replace the image placeholders with real images

Example filenames:
- christ-pantocrator.jpg
- virgin-and-child.png
- saint-george.jpg
- archangel-michael.webp

The gallery is configured to display these images beautifully with category filtering and modal views.
